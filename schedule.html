PHASE 3 — 3-DAY FIELD EXPEDITION ITINERARY

55 continuous hours in the hills of Kerala designed to push your prototype from theoretical circuit schematics to deployable, field-tested cyber-physical hardware.

~55 hrs
Total Programme

Friday 9:00 AM through Sunday 4:00 PM IST continuous flow

36 hrs
Continuous Hack Sprint

Begins Friday midnight; clock runs nonstop through meals

8.5 hrs
Workshop Hours

Set A: 3 hrs (Foundation) · Set B: 5.5 hrs (Hands-On Labs)

3 Talks
IEEE HTB Sessions

Inaugural address, HTB Committee breakout & volunteer keynote

📝 BrainStorm Planning Note: Humanitarian Learning Report (20–25% of Total Score)

The BrainStorm Round produces the Humanitarian Learning Report, which carries 20–25% of your final evaluation score. Rather than locking participants into an artificial classroom slot, the schedule accommodates report synthesis as a rolling task during the continuous hack sprint. Dedicated Saturday evening hours (07:00 PM – 09:00 PM) are recommended for team synthesis with mentors.

DAY 1 · FRIDAY

ARRIVAL, FOUNDATION & HACK KICKOFF

09:00 AM – Midnight Hack Start
09:00 AM – 10:30 AM

Registration & Hardware Kit Distribution

Team basecamp check-in, credential issuance, component inspection, and workstation allotment.

10:30 AM – 11:30 AM

Inaugural Ceremony & IEEE HTB Keynote

Welcome address by IEEE SPS Kerala Chapter leadership and opening keynote on humanitarian technology pathways.

11:45 AM – 01:15 PM

Workshop Set A — Session 1: GIS & Weather Intelligence (1.5 hrs)

Geospatial hazard mapping, multi-spectral satellite layers, and offline map caching for field deployment.

01:15 PM – 02:15 PM

Networking Lunch & Informal Mentor Meet

Shared meal with mentors and organizing committee.

02:15 PM – 03:45 PM

Workshop Set A — Session 2: Offline-First Architectures (1.5 hrs)

Mesh networking protocols, CRDT peer-to-peer data sync without cellular infrastructure, and progressive offline UX.

05:30 PM – 08:30 PM

★ Mentor Networking Night & Table Rotations

SDG-themed table rotations with industry, academic, and humanitarian domain mentors (25-min rounds).

12:00 Midnight (Sharp)

🚀 36-Hour Continuous Hack Sprint Kickoff!

Hack clocks start! Clocks run continuously without stopping for meals.

DAY 2 · SATURDAY

DEEP BUILDING, HANDS-ON LABS & AUDITS

Continuous Sprint Round-the-Clock
09:30 AM – 01:00 PM

Workshop Set B — Hands-On Lab 1: Embedded & Edge AI (3.5 hrs)

Hands-on laboratory sessions interfacing sensor buses, flash flashing, model quantization, and edge inference.

02:00 PM – 04:00 PM

Workshop Set B — Hands-On Lab 2: Fabrication & Robotics (2.0 hrs)

Rapid 3D printing queue, laser cutting enclosures, actuator testing, and fail-safe safety integration.

04:30 PM – 06:30 PM

Mid-Sprint Checkpoint & Red-Team Audit

Dual technical + humanitarian mentors inspect progress, test fail-safes, and course-correct prototypes.

07:00 PM – 09:00 PM

BrainStorm Round: Report Writing Session

Focused documentation sprint synthesizing humanitarian insights and ethics into the scored report.

10:00 PM – Dawn

Overnight Prototyping & Stress Testing

Continuous building with round-the-clock mentor and fabrication support.

DAY 3 · SUNDAY

SPRINT FINISH, GRAND JURY DEFENSE & AWARDS

08:00 AM – 04:30 PM Finale
12:00 Noon (Sharp)

🛑 HACK SPRINT ENDS — Hard Code & Hardware Freeze

All 36 continuous hack hours concluded. System lock.

01:00 PM – 03:00 PM

★ Grand Jury Demonstration & Defense Expo

Jury walkthrough, adversarial testing, live environmental stress-testing, and Q&A defense.

03:15 PM – 04:15 PM

Valedictory Ceremony, IEEE SPS Awards & Closing

Announcement of winners, track champions, citation honors, and mentor acknowledgements.
